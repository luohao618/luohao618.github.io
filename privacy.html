Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

PPCOS Local is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses Google OAuth to access the Google Ads API on behalf of the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

Read access to Google Ads account data (campaigns, keywords, ads, conversions and performance metrics) for accounts the owner manages.

How data is used

Data is used only to produce reports and analysis for the owner. It is stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
